Cookie Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. They let a site remember your choices and help advertisers show relevant ads and measure how they perform. Similar technologies, such as local storage and pixels, work in a similar way and are covered by this policy too.

TypeProviderPurposeConsent needed?
Strictly necessaryThis site / consent toolRemembers your cookie choices and keeps the site secure.No

Google advertising cookies typically have names such as IDE, __gads, __gpi and NID, and they can last from a few months up to about two years. Google lists the cookies it uses on its cookie information page.

Your choices

  • Consent banner: visitors from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see a consent message on their first visit. You can accept, reject or customise your choices, and change them at any time via the privacy settings link at the bottom of the page.
  • Google's ad settings: turn personalised ads on or off in My Ad Center.
  • Industry opt-outs: aboutads.info (US) and youronlinechoices.eu (EU).
  • Browser settings: you can block or delete cookies in your browser. If you block all cookies, you may still see ads, but they will be less relevant.
