Contact us

Questions, corrections or a favourite beach we missed? We read every message.

Email

The fastest way to reach us is by email: webcreatorlh@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2–3 working days.

What to write to us about

  • Corrections: a price, a timetable or a beach that has changed since our last update.
  • Suggestions: beaches, towns or topics you would like us to cover.
  • Questions: we can't plan your whole trip, but we're happy to point you in the right direction.
  • Photo credits: if you are the author of a photo on this site and want a credit changed, let us know.
Please note: we are an independent information site, not a travel agency. We can't make bookings, issue refunds or answer questions about reservations made with third parties.

Privacy

We only use your email address to reply to you, and we never share it. See our Privacy Policy for details.
